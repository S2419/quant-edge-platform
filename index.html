We also bet, in public

Sport is the harshest scoring environment we could find. Every forecast gets a price attached, a closing line to measure against, and a result inside ninety minutes.

So we bet. Not because tipping is the business, but because a bet is a forecast with money and a timestamp on it. This is a working track record, not a finished one: 157 bets is suggestive, 500+ is what significance looks like.

What we do
  • Publish single bets above gate, sized to edge

What we don’t
  • Accumulator bait or “banker of the day”
  • 96% accuracy claims
  • Celebrity endorsements (real or AI-faked)
  • “The AI does the work” mystique. A model produces numbers, a human decides
  • Tipping. We're not tipsters. We're a multi-predictive intelligence system reading predictive markets
