Quant Lab · Team cards

Every club, rated 1 to 20 by how it plays

What the 1 to 20 ratings mean. Each rating is where a club ranks against the other clubs in its own division, last season (2025/26), league games only. 20 is the top 5% of the division on that measure, 16 to 20 the top quarter, 1 to 5 the bottom quarter. A promoted club is rated on the division it played in last season.

264 clubs across 14 divisions, with this season's table, the next league game, the squad from our player cards and who is listed out.

  • Hover or tap a rating for the real number behind it.
  • Greyed ratings barely carry over from one season to the next. How a team plays (possession, passing) holds strongly; results only partly.
  • Tap a player to open his card, in the squad or on the pitch of his club's last starting XI.
  • Which formation works best? Mostly we can't tell. Across all clubs, of 543 shapes used 6+ times, no more stood out from what our ratings expected than chance alone would give. So every card says "can't tell yet", and that is the honest answer.

    What the cards can't tell you

    • Ratings are last season's. A new manager or a big summer changes a team. This season's table sits on the card for that reason.
    • A 15 is a rank, not a level. A 15 in the Scottish Premiership is not the same standard as a 15 in the Premier League.
    • Expected goals ratings appear only where our feed has them for 90%+ of a club's games.
    • The squad list shows players with 900+ league minutes last season in our 14 divisions who are at the club this season. New arrivals from elsewhere are not on it yet.
    • No warning never means fit. Our feed has no injury news for 7 of the 14 leagues, and a club's list for the next game may not be out yet. Status from 13 October 2026.

    Data sources

    • API-Football: match statistics, scores, the official table, fixtures and injury news.
    • Ratings, carry-over tests and formation records: our own, from that data.
    • Club badges are the clubs' own marks, shown to identify them.
