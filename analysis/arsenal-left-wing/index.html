Arsenal's left wing: what's missing, and three January targets

Update, 18 October 2026. Our shots forecasts now adjust for the league a player moves from. We added this to our transfer system after it beat the plain forecast on moves it never saw. The gain is small: average error 0.360 to 0.349 shots per 90 on 389 moves into 2025/26, a cut of 0.011 (95% interval 0.003 to 0.020), about 3%. The first version of this piece, dated 15 October, did not adjust, and said so. A move from a smaller top league, such as Belgium's, into the big five costs 18% of shots on average, so our forecast for Tzolis drops from 2.2 to 1.8 shots per 90. The three targets move between big-five leagues, which costs 2% on average. Their shots forecasts dip slightly, and the three, their order and the club-cut table are unchanged. Without the selling filters, Vinícius Júnior now edges Godts for first; the same three names.

Last season Arsenal's left matched or beat the top six on shots, dribbles and goals + assists. Both men who played there have gone, and Tzolis, who replaced them, is out with a hamstring injury. Of 119 left wingers, three pass our filters and project, on average, to replace what left. One costs less than Tzolis did.

Our answer.

  • Under a Tzolis-sized fee: Derry Scherhant, Freiburg, 24. Reported price about £14m to £15m.
  • A stretch: Antonio Nusa, RB Leipzig, 21. Reported at around £60m, and Leipzig reportedly won't sell mid-season.
  • Best player, wrong January: Kenan Yıldız, Juventus, 21. Injured until about the end of November, valued at €100m to €150m.

Nusa and Scherhant pass at every club cut we tried. How many make it, and who, depends on where we draw that line: at 15, Jesús Rodríguez comes in and Scherhant drops to fourth; at 25, Yıldız drops out and only two pass. See How we picked.

What the left had, and has

Last season Leandro Trossard made 21 starts on the left and Gabriel Martinelli 11. Between them they matched the left wingers of the Premier League's top six on shots and beat them on dribbles and goals + assists. Trossard went to Besiktas for £17m and Martinelli to Al-Hilal for £60m. Christos Tzolis came from Club Brugge for a reported £34m and has started 5 of 6 league games on the left. Noni Madueke started the other.

That is far too few to judge him on.

So what is missing? Two things. A second left winger: there is one natural left winger in the squad now, where there were two, and he is out. Tzolis went off with a hamstring injury playing for Greece in the October break, and Arteta says he will miss a few weeks. And so far, end product: Tzolis is taking 0.7 shots per 90, well below our forecast of 1.8, which allows for the step up from Belgium. That forecast is for a full season and 365 minutes is not one. Either way, the left has no fallback.

How we picked

We wrote the method down before looking at a single name. The test as written found no output gap last season, so no one passed it. That is an honest answer, but it was about last season. Everything below answers the follow-up we declared after seeing that: who could replace the output that left? Our registered price rule, the fees paid for similar players, failed a sanity check (it priced Yıldız at £4.5m), so we use reported prices instead.

  1. 119 left wingers. Listed as a left winger by our lineups last season, or 10+ starts on the left; 900+ league minutes; under 27 on 1 January 2027; playing this season; in our 14 divisions.
  2. 27 for whom Arsenal is a natural next step. Arsenal ranks in the top quarter of every club he could move to, on our destination score. Tested on 920 real moves: the club a player actually joined ranked 82% of the way up on average. Here it runs from his current club, which that test did not cover.
  3. 8 at clubs that sell in January. We dropped players their club signed this summer (only 90 of 1,280 January sales from 2023 to 2026 were) and players at the 20 highest-rated clubs in our ratings. These two filters were added after we declared the follow-up. Without them the top three would be Vinícius Júnior, M. Godts, J. Doku, none of whom their clubs would sell in January.
  4. 3 whose projection, averaged over the four measures, is above what left. Shots, key passes, dribbles and goals + assists, each against what Arsenal's left produced last season. Dribbles do most of the work: Scherhant projects below last season's left on shots and key passes.

Where we draw the club line changes the list. Juventus is the 22nd-rated club, just outside the top-20 cut. Como and Brighton are inside it.

On the edge: Jesús Rodríguez

Como's left winger, 21 on 1 January 2027. His projection averages 1.17 times what Arsenal's left produced, above Nusa and Scherhant, and he passes every filter except one: Como are the 17th-rated club in our ratings, inside the top-20 cut. Draw the line at 15 and he is in, second, ahead of Nusa, which pushes Scherhant out of the three. Como paid Betis €22m for him in 2025 on a contract to 2030, and in March reports put Como's price around €55m (BeSoccer, TransferFeed).

Money: what fits the rules

From this season the Premier League caps squad costs (wages, transfer fees spread over the contract, and agents' fees) at 85% of revenue. It checks on 1 March, straight after the January window. UEFA's version, which applies to clubs in Europe, is stricter at 70%. In 2024/25 Arsenal's revenue was £690.3m and wages £346.8m. With fee spreading of about £172m (our figure, derived from the published accounts), wages and fees came to about 75% of revenue on a simple basis. The rules count differently: Greg Cordell estimates Arsenal at about 62% on UEFA's measure.

  • 1% of Arsenal's revenue is about £6.9m a year.
  • Scherhant's reported price adds about £3.1m to £3.3m a year in fee. Nusa's about £11m to £13m. Yıldız's £19m to £28m.
  • We have no wage data, so every figure here is a floor. Wages come on top.
  • We can't compute Arsenal's room under the rules exactly: the 2025/26 accounts are not out, and summer 2026 brought £77m in from Martinelli and Trossard, and new signings including Tzolis.

What this can't tell you

  • Whether a club will sell. Our filters use what clubs have done in January before. Leipzig and Juventus say no, and the prices above are reported, not ours.
  • Wages and contracts. We don't have them.
  • Fit with Arteta's system. Scherhant in particular is a different player from Trossard.
  • Our forecast for Tzolis is well above what he has done so far. Same model, a few games in. If he settles, the need is depth, not a starter.
  • This is a follow-up, not the registered test. The registered test found no output gap.
  • The top-20 cut is a judgement call. Juventus sits just outside it, and moving it five places either way changes who makes the three, and how many.
