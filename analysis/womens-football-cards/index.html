Quant Lab · Women's football

Every player and club, rated 1 to 20 by what they actually did

What the 1 to 20 ratings mean. Each rating is where a player ranks against everyone in her position in her own league that season, with 900+ league minutes. A club ranks against the other clubs in its league. 20 is the top 5% on that measure, 16 to 20 the top quarter, 1 to 5 the bottom quarter.

705 players and 68 clubs across the WSL, Liga F, Serie A Women, the Frauen-Bundesliga and the Premiere Ligue.

  • Rated on the latest full season we can. That is 2025/26 where a player reached 900 minutes in the games our feed has, otherwise 2024/25. Each card says which.
  • Hover or tap a rating for the real number behind it.
  • Greyed ratings barely carry over from one season to the next. We tested every rating on the same players and clubs in two seasons running.
  • A 15 is a rank, not a level. A 15 in Serie A Women and a 15 in the WSL mean the same place in different leagues, not the same standard.

    What the cards can't tell you

    • Our feed misses some games. It has player numbers for about 87% of league games in 2023/24, 78% in 2024/25 and 59% in 2025/26 (Liga F aside). Minutes and totals on a card count only the games it has, so they can be lower than a player's real total.
    • Liga F has no player numbers in our feed since 2025/26. Liga F players are rated on 2024/25. Liga F clubs are rated on 2025/26 results only, except Valencia (not in Liga F that season, rated on 2024/25) and Alavés (no rating).
    • Club ratings leave out possession, corners and expected goals. They are built from results and the players' own numbers added up per game.
    • Defending is counted, not judged. Tackles, interceptions and blocks are counts, so a defender in a side that keeps the ball makes fewer of them.
    • Assists swing from season to season. "Final ball" barely carries over for attackers and defenders, and only partly for midfielders.
    • League games only, no goalkeepers, no injury news.

    Data sources

    • Player photos and club badges belong to their owners and are shown to identify them.
    • API-Football: player match statistics, scores, fixtures and league tables. Results to 9 November 2026.
    • Ratings and carry-over tests: our own, from that data.
