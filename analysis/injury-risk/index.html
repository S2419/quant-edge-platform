Injury risk 2026/27: who is most likely to miss five games

Who is most likely to miss five or more league games through injury this season? The best guide is last season's record, and it is a guide, not a verdict.

  • What drives it. Injury games last season, by a distance. Age and position barely move it. Heavy league minutes do not push risk up. The study behind this.
  • How good is it? Tested on a season it never saw, the model scored about 6% better than using each league's average rate. Real, but modest: plenty of high-risk players stay fit, and some low-risk ones don't.

How it works

An injury game is a league fixture a player missed for a medical reason. Suspensions, illness, international duty and "doubtful" are left out. Outfield players with 600 or more league minutes last season who are in the same league this season. The model was fitted on 5,133 player-seasons, 2022/23 to 2024/25, each followed into the next season.

Risk comes from one model across all seven leagues. Leagues log injuries differently: in the Eredivisie's own records 14% of regulars missed five or more, and in the Championship 38%. Read the figures as a ranking within each league more than an exact level.

What it can't see

Fixture congestion, cup and European minutes, and anything that happened outside these seven leagues. A player new to the league starts with no record here.
