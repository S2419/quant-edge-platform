We tested ourselves on summer 2024

It sorts signings well. It gets the expensive ones wrong, and we think we know why.

  • 1,553 summer-2024 signings in our 14 divisions. The fifth we rated most likely to become regulars did it 48% of the time. The fifth we rated least likely: 6%.
  • League moves: our shots range held for 311 of 405 players who changed league (77%). It held for moves across and down. It failed again for players stepping up from a second division.
  • The 20 biggest fees: 10 right. No better than a coin flip. 7 of the 10 misses went the same way: we said he wouldn't play, he did.

The rules

Only what was known on 1 June 2024. The signing model learned from the summer 2023 window alone. Club strength is our own rating, as it stood that day. Loans made permanent don't count as signings: the player was already there.

A regular means he played at least half of his new club's league minutes after he arrived. Loaned out again in January means he wasn't a regular.

The 20 cases are the 20 biggest reported fees among those signings. Chosen by that rule, every one shown, misses included.

Why we got the big ones wrong

The model doesn't know a fee was paid. It learned from 1,541 signings in the summer 2023 window, and only 71 of them cost €20m or more. The rest were loans, free transfers and cheaper moves, where a step up to a bigger club usually means fewer minutes. Clubs that pay big fees usually mean to play the player: 12 of these 20 became regulars.

  • Olise to Bayern: we said 21%, he played 77%.
  • Pedro Neto to Chelsea: we said 16%, he played 67%.
  • Doué to PSG: we said 16%, he played 58%.

The fix isn't simple. Our data feed stopped publishing fee amounts in the 2025 window, so a fee can't go into the model. And on 2024, where we do have fees, adding one helped a little, but not by enough to be sure. We tried the next best thing, the player's form and the strength of the club he left. It didn't pass on a season the model hadn't seen, so it isn't in.

Until something does, we say it out loud: for paid moves into the clubs at the top of our ratings, our number runs low. By 4 points in 2024. By 14 in 2025.
